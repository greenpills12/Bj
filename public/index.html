Blackjack rules

  • Six decks. Dealer stands on all 17s and checks for blackjack with an Ace or ten showing.
  • Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Other wins pay 1 to 1. A push returns your bet.
  • Double on your first two cards. Split equal-value cards into up to four hands per spot. Split Aces get one card each.
  • Insurance is offered against an Ace, costs half your bet and pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack.
  • Blackjack Classic has three betting spots. Choose a spot, then tap chips to bet.

Simulation Mode

The bank panel sets the win and push rates. A sampled win becomes a loss if the bank cannot cover it. A push returns the stake. Cards are randomly drawn to match the resulting outcome.
